This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising, comments, forms or accounts, and it loads nothing
from other services. The only personal data involved in a visit is your IP address, which the hosting
provider logs.

## Who is responsible

Markus Brigl, Italy, runs this website privately and is the data controller. You can reach me at
[privacy@hivevm.org](mailto:privacy@hivevm.org).

## Hosting by GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA) and GitHub B.V. (Netherlands).
To deliver a page, your browser has to connect to GitHub's servers. According to GitHub's documentation,
your IP address is logged and stored for security purposes when you visit a GitHub Pages site. I have no
access to these logs and do not use them.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. There is a legitimate interest in delivering this website securely.
- **Transfer outside the EU:** the data may be processed in the USA. GitHub states that it has certified
  to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists
  (Art. 45 GDPR), and that it generally uses standard contractual clauses for other transfers.
- **Retention:** GitHub decides how long it keeps this data and does not publish a fixed period. Its
  statement says this depends on the purpose and on legal obligations.

Details are in the
[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Theme setting

If you choose a colour theme with the buttons in the navigation, your browser stores that choice locally
under `hivevm-theme` so that it applies on your next visit. It is never sent anywhere. If you do not use
the buttons, nothing is stored.

## Fonts

The fonts are served from this website itself, so loading them contacts no other service.

## Links to other websites

Links to other websites, most of them to GitHub, only connect you to those websites when you follow them.
Their own privacy policies then apply.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict its processing, and to
object to its processing (Art. 15–18 and 21 GDPR). I cannot identify you from your IP address and hold no
logs, so for the data described above, requests are best addressed to GitHub at privacy@github.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In Italy, this
is the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/).
